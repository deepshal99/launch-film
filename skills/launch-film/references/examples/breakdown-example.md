# Example breakdown — a 20 s fintech app launch ad (16:9)


**Format:** 1920x1080, 60 fps, 20.0 s (1201 frames). Music around 133.5 BPM (one beat is about 27 frames). It's a minimal white canvas with a saturated brand-colour floor glow across the bottom ~30%.

## Timing map (seconds)
| t | beat |
|---|---|
| 0.0–1.6 | App icon alone, centred, gently breathing. A halftone dot field blooms radially from about 0.4 s |
| 1.6–2.0 | The icon morphs (stretch + gradient shift) into a two-state pill toggle. A hand cursor enters |
| 2.0–3.8 | The cursor hovers and taps. The dot field clears to the edges, then pulses in a second colour at the tap |
| 3.9–5.2 | Toggle flips. A burst of 3D objects arcs overhead left→right, with heavy motion blur and near objects huge and defocused |
| 5.2–5.4 | One object fills the lens (whip transition) |
| 5.4–7.4 | Two-line kinetic headline. Words arrive one at a time with blur-rise; the second line is smaller. Objects drift in depth behind |
| 7.0–7.8 | Headline defocuses out (round blur ramp) |
| 7.8–10.0 | UI atoms (chips, nav bar, FAB) pop in, a phone body grows under them, then the camera pulls back so they sit inside a phone |
| 10.0–11.8 | Sentence carousel "Verb [icon slot] noun": the noun + icon swap every ~0.25 s, accelerating into a blurred strip of icons, landing on "anything" |
| 11.8–13.2 | "with [icon] noun": the icon rolls sideways into a pill, and a small overline word types in above it |
| 13.3–14.2 | The pill drops into a card: amount types in, sub-value fades, the cursor taps a CTA |
| 14.2–15.2 | Extruded 3D rings fly through the camera. A 3D extruded stat glyph tumbles in and settles flat into an outline style |
| 15.2–16.0 | Stat sentence holds with a footnote and a dot-field bloom |
| 16.0–16.6 | Side words slide outward with horizontal smear; a phone rises from the floor glow |
| 16.6–20.0 | End line typed word by word above a blurred phone, an app banner card sits on the phone, and it holds to the end |

## Motion grammar worth reusing
- **Transitions:** every scene hands off through a blur (defocus, directional smear or whip). No crossfades.
- **Typography:** words land one by one on beats (blur 12→0 px, rise ~20 px, ease-out).
- **Recurring texture:** halftone dot fields bloom radially and clear from the centre on accents.
- **3D objects:** depth of field (near = big + blurred), motion blur along the path, tumbling spin.
- **UI:** built from atoms first, then the device frame arrives around them.
- **Accent:** one strong colour for the floor glow, near-black ink for type, white for everything else.
